Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our Clinic FrontDesk ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with our platform and WhatsApp integration services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following information to facilitate clinic appointments and patient management:

  • Patient Contact Information: Name, phone number, and basic demographic data provided during clinic registration.
  • Appointment Information: Appointment dates, times, clinic locations, and status (e.g., Scheduled, Confirmed, Canceled).
  • WhatsApp Communications: Inbound and outbound message text strictly related to appointment reminders, confirmations, and scheduling updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for administrative operational purposes, including:

  • Sending automated WhatsApp reminders for upcoming medical appointments.
  • Processing patient responses (such as CONFIRM or CANCEL) to update clinic schedules in real time.
  • Allowing authorized clinic staff to manage daily appointment flows.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or patient data to any third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only transmitted through secure protocols to external providers essential for delivering service:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API: Phone numbers and message contents are transmitted to process WhatsApp notifications under Meta's platform policies.

We implement technical and organizational measures (including HTTPS encryption and restricted database access) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is retained only as long as necessary to maintain active clinic scheduling records.
