Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clinic FrontDesk ("we", "us", or "our") processes information when clinics use our appointment-management platform and WhatsApp integration.

Clinic FrontDesk is intended for administrative appointment management and patient communication. It is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or emergency services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how a clinic uses the platform, we may process:

  • Patient contact information: such as name and phone number.
  • Appointment information: such as clinic, doctor, service, date, time, and appointment status.
  • WhatsApp communications: inbound and outbound messages related to appointment reminders, confirmations, cancellations, and rescheduling.
  • Operational records: such as appointment events, reminder status, and audit records needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Manage appointments and clinic schedules.
  • Send appointment reminders and scheduling notifications.
  • Process patient responses such as CONFIRM, CANCEL, or RESCHEDULE.
  • Help authorized clinic staff manage appointment workflows.
  • Maintain service security, reliability, and audit records.

3. WhatsApp and Service Providers

Clinic FrontDesk may use the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API to send and receive appointment-related communications. Information such as phone numbers and message content may therefore be transmitted through Meta's infrastructure as required to deliver WhatsApp messaging.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or patient information for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Role of Clinics

Clinics using Clinic FrontDesk are responsible for the patient information they enter into the platform and for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis to collect, use, and communicate with patients regarding appointments.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss. These measures may include HTTPS encryption, access controls, authentication, restricted database access, logging, and backup procedures.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain clinic appointment records, meet operational and security needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may also depend on the policies of the clinic using Clinic FrontDesk.

7. Access, Correction, and Deletion

Patients may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed through Clinic FrontDesk. In most cases, requests should first be directed to the clinic with which the patient has an appointment.

8. Medical Disclaimer

Clinic FrontDesk and its WhatsApp messaging features are intended only for administrative scheduling and notification purposes. They do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, or clinical decision-making.
